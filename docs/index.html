Author

Tianqi Ouyang

Published

Project Overview

Clinical research analysis of long-term kidney outcomes after immune checkpoint inhibitor (ICI) therapy — CKD incidence, progression, and ESKD over 10-year follow-up.

This website contains three components:

  • Analysis — Main cohort analysis (full pipeline + models)
  • Treated Group — ICI-treated cohort data build + Table 1
  • Control Group — Non-ICI cancer control cohort data build + Table 1
  • Matching — Propensity-score matching of treated vs control with balance diagnostics
  • Results — Outcome analyses on matched cohort: CIF, Fine-Gray, cause-specific Cox, 5-yr logistic for all CKD/ESKD outcomes

Inclusion / Exclusion Criteria

Treated Group

Step Criterion
1 ICI-treated patients from source Excel
2 last_cre_days > 0 (follow-up after index)
3 Non-missing baseline CRE (pre_median_CRE_90days)
4 eGFR_CRE_baseline >= 10

Control Group

Step Criterion
1 Unique EMPIs from RPDR Dem.txt
2 Has neoplasm Dx (ICD10 C00-D49) for Index_date
3 last_cre_days > 0
4 Baseline CRE within 12 months prior to Index_date
5 Follow-up >= 18 months (548 days)
6 >= 2 CRE results after Index_date
7 Non-missing baseline CRE
8 eGFR_CRE_baseline >= 10

Key Conventions

  • All time anchored on Index_date
  • eGFR: CKD-EPI 2021 creatinine-only (race-free)
  • Yearly windows: year X = days (X*365 - 90) to (X*365) post-index
  • CKD outcomes require sustained >= 90 days
  • Competing risk priority: Death(2) > CKD(1) > Censored(0)
  • death_Xyear = 1 strictly means patient died within X years
